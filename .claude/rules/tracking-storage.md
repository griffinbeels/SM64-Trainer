## The arm-position gate

A matched start trigger arms only if the segment could still be RUN from where the event left Mario (`can_run_from`), from three definition-derived rules and no world-edge table: (A) the next required step must be firable from here (`fires_from`, `_PRECONDITION_PARAM`); (B) an unpinned `level_exit` from a non-castle level must land in `CASTLE_LEVELS`; (C) the arm must not stand on every end trigger's destination. `game_reset` is exempt. Pinned by `tests/test_defaults_corpus.py::test_a_menu_warp_into_a_course_arms_no_movement` and `tests/test_segments.py`.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-arm-position-gate](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-arm-position-gate).

## Split and merge

`split_definition`/`merge_definitions` are pure authoring ops. Split inherits `match_mode` and refuses 2+ waypoints or an `unfireable` half; merge keeps the seam as a waypoint and refuses a pair that does not meet at castle-subarea resolution. Results carry no `seed_key`; inputs survive untouched. Wired via `service.split_segment`/`merge_segments`.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#split-and-merge](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#split-and-merge).

## The target queue

The held target is a FIFO of detections (`Projector._target_hooked`). A PICKED head (his click) keeps every pre-queue rule and clears the queue. A HOOKED head is exempt from origin retirement and pops on success, forfeits on a real anchor in a foreign course, expires on `hold_budget`, drops on `game_reset`/`session_started` — never on a mere disarm. **An ambiguous burst detects nothing: only a lone eligible arm queues.** Only defs `hooks_on_arm` accepts enter; promotion takes the oldest survivor and may replace a leftover hand (`_hand_is_a_leftover`), never a click. A stale hooked head yields to a valid star grab (`_pending_grab_take`) and to a fill naming a segment armed now (`_declines_fill`: the arena's fight, whatever order it lands in beside the arrival reset — `tests/test_arena_fill_race.py`); a pick never does. All journal-derived. Measure with `tools/measure_target_queue.py`; pinned by `tests/test_target_queue.py`.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-target-queue](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-target-queue).

## The star owns the hand

Explicit user choices take priority; arrival selects nothing. (1) A piece whose parent can hold the hand (`speaks_through_a_parent`) never hooks or queues — click only. (2) A piece arming never suspends its parent star's target. (3) A piece's success follows onto its parent only when `segments.attributable_parent` finds exactly one entity across identically-firing enabled defs; otherwise leave the hand, and an ambiguous piece conjures no parent card. (4) A PICKED piece survives its parent's grab; a HOOKED one yields. Pinned by `tests/test_family_dance.py`. Subarea narrowing is retired (`.claude/rules/ui-selector.md`).
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-star-owns-the-hand](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#the-star-owns-the-hand).

## Attempt ordering must use journal_id, never the raw id

A reattributed attempt keeps its SEGMENT-namespace id, so raw-id order is wrong forever. Sort by `projection.journal_id(id)` everywhere: `db.attempts()` (the one list every consumer reads), `views._attempt_json` stamps `journal_id` for the client (never duplicate `SEGMENT_ATTEMPT_OFFSET` in JS), `practice.js::comparator`, `rankpage.js` `EntityDetail`, `service._newest_attempt_id`. Pinned by `tests/test_tracker_service.py::test_newest_attempt_id_ignores_the_segment_namespace_offset`.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#attempt-ordering-must-use-journal_id-never-the-raw-id](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#attempt-ordering-must-use-journal_id-never-the-raw-id).

## A movement ends at the entrance, not at the course load

Castle movements end on `entrance_touched {to}` (where the entrance leads); `warp_entered {level}` is the legacy pipe entries only. `topology.entrance_level(to)` is THE door. A new condition needs a row in `TRIGGER_JOURNAL_TYPES`, `_SYNTH_PARAMS` and `_CLAUSE_BUILDERS`. **A repair may fix the thing it is about; it may not spend the user's own work doing it** (v21: end clause only, `seed_dirty` untouched). `step_node` must resolve a destination-carrying clause, never None. `projection.warp_destinations` is a required pre-pass, derived and never written back. A menu warp completes no movement, by design.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-movement-ends-at-the-entrance-not-at-the-course-load](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-movement-ends-at-the-entrance-not-at-the-course-load).

## A failure only names what he chose, and winning ends everything

A non-success closure records only when `self.target` names that def (`_untargeted_failure_for_segment`); successes always record. A human label is a journal event (`strat_overrides`), never a `strat_tag` default. On the grand `key_grabbed`, `feed` ends by silently cancelling every armed def, LAST so Bowser 3's success records.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-failure-only-names-what-he-chose-and-winning-ends-everything](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-failure-only-names-what-he-chose-and-winning-ends-everything).

## A pause exit is not a retry

`SegmentEngine._arrived_by_a_real_move` gates the anchor rewind of BOTH `_feed_waypoint` and `_feed_loose`: a co-frame anchor that walked a real world edge is not a retry; a menu warp (off-graph) is. `frames_since_warp_op` does NOT separate them. Deliberately narrow: `_anchor_echo` itself is not widened.
Detail: [docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-pause-exit-is-not-a-retry](../../docs/rules/tracking-storage.md#a-pause-exit-is-not-a-retry).
