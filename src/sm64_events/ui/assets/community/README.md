# Sign-in assets

Discord Clyde is the official white mark from https://discord.com/branding.
Google G and Google Sans come from the approved sign-in branding kit:
https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
Twitch Glitch is the unmodified white SVG from the official brand archive:
https://brand.twitch.com/uploads/Twitch-Brand.zip
Archive member: `Twitch Brand/Twitch Logos/02. Glitch/04. White/glitch_flat_white.svg`.
Preserve the marks' proportions and provider colors. Google Sans's bundled
license is google-sans-OFL.txt. These files are provider assets, not app artwork.
